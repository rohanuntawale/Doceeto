癒 Iyashihow we operate11
how we operate, legally and on the ground

We have done the homework most skip.

Built to be compliant

  • Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020: registered doctors only; consults, prescriptions and records follow the RMP framework.
  • CDSCO and pharmacy rules: prescription-required (Schedule H / H1) medicine dispensed only against a valid prescription, via licensed partners.
  • DPDP Act, 2023: health data treated as sensitive, consent-first, encrypted, and portable via ABDM.

How we start, density first

Emergency, doctors and delivery all reward density. So we do not launch across India. We win one neighbourhood, then repeat the playbook.
